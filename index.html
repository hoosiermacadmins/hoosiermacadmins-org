★ Indiana's Apple IT Community

Mac Admins across Indiana, connected.

Hoosier Mac Admins is a free community for IT professionals who manage Apple devices for businesses, schools, nonprofits, and government across Indiana. Join us for quarterly meetups, hands-on talks, and honest conversation with people who get it.

📍 Meetups across Central Indiana 🗓️ Typically once a quarter 🎤 Open to speakers & sponsors
What we're about

A close-knit group of people who manage Apple devices for a living

Whether you're troubleshooting MDM policies, rolling out new hardware, or just want to talk shop with someone who understands the job, this is your crew.

🤝

Quarterly meetups

We aim to meet the second Tuesday of most quarters, mostly in person with speakers & sponsors.

🍻

Happy hours & socials

Periodic no-agenda hangouts — cookouts, Topgolf, food halls — so the community can connect without a formal presentation.

💬

Slack community

Ask questions, share wins, and troubleshoot with fellow Hoosier Mac Admins any day of the week, not just at meetups.
