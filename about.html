★ About Us

A community built by and for Indiana Mac admins

Hoosier Mac Admins is a close-knit community of professionals who manage Apple devices for businesses, non-profits, and government entities across Indiana.

Hoosier Mac Admins, an Indiana-based Mac Admin group, serves as a close-knit community of dedicated professionals who specialize in managing Apple devices for a diverse array of organizations, including businesses, non-profits, and government entities. Our primary objective is to foster collaboration and support among like-minded individuals in the field.

Through regular meetups (virtually and in-person) and open discussions, this group endeavors to create a space where members can freely exchange insights, best practices, and troubleshooting tips — all crucial to their daily roles. By sharing expertise and experiences, Hoosier Mac Admins plays a pivotal role in enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of Apple device management across Indiana workplaces.

Social media

We share information and connect with our colleagues using the following channels:

  • Slack: #hoosier_macadmins on the MacAdmins Slack workspace
  • Google Calendar: Hoosier Mac Admins Meetup Calendar

Meetups

We aim to hold meetings once a quarter, mostly in person. We hope to add additional virtual meetings, when it makes sense. These plans are subject to change. During meetings, we may have one or two presenters and/or sponsors.

Happy hours & social events

Periodically we organize happy hours or general social outings so members of the community can socialize and commiserate without a more formal, presentation-style meeting. Past venues have included Topgolf Fishers, The Garage Food Hall, and local parks for cookouts.

Speaking & hosting

We are always open to adding more hosts, sponsors, and presenters to our community.

  • If you or your organization would like to sponsor an event, please email hoosiermacad@gmail.com.
  • If you or your organization would like to host a meetup, please fill out our hosting form.
  • If you would like to present at a future meetup, please fill out our speaker form.
